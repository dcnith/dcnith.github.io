Research Highlights

Selected Impact

Electric Cooking Programme — UKAid/MECS, Bihar

Led supply-chain activation across 10 blocks. 12,000+ households reached; 1,505 paying customers converted to electric cooking. Co-authored the MECS Phase II public report shaping national scale-up recommendations.

Cooking Behaviour Change — Nudging Experiment

Designed a 3-round nudging experiment (n = 194 households). Found 26 min/day reduction in biomass use and 31 min/day in LPG use. Showed in-person facilitation significantly outperforms SMS nudges.

Willingness-to-Pay for Solar Lamps

Panel data from 1,159 rural households across 4 waves; logistic regression with village fixed/random effects identifying WTP determinants.

Published in Renewable & Sustainable Energy Reviews · IF 16.8
Solar Energy & Women's Empowerment

4-round longitudinal study (n = 313, 4 states) quantifying pathways from solar access to skills, occupation, and women's agency.

Published in Energy Research & Social Science · IF 6.7
Education Outcomes — Solar Study Lamps

873-household study finding ~30 min/day improvement in children's study time from solar lamp access.

Published in Energy for Sustainable Development · IF 5.7
Current AI Work

AI & GenAI Projects

LLM Fine-Tuning — Fine-tuned DeBERTa-v3 Large/Base using SFT + PEFT/LoRA with pseudo-labelling, stratified K-fold validation, probability calibration, and weighted ensembling; achieved 91% accuracy on harmful content classification benchmark.
Hybrid Log Classification Pipeline — Multi-tier system (Regex → Sentence Transformers + LR → LLM fallback) that improved accuracy by 40% and cut operational costs by 30% over rule-based baselines.
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) — Designing knowledge retrieval pipelines for structured and unstructured document corpora.
Agentic AI Workflows — Multi-step reasoning systems using LangGraph orchestration and tool-use frameworks.
Medical Imaging (Deep Learning) — CT scan abdominal trauma detection and skin lesion classification (SIIM-ISIC) via ResNet50 transfer learning on Kaggle datasets.

Research & Applied Work

Projects

Research Projects at IIT Bombay

Enabling Electric Cooking Ecosystem for Rural India
December 2022 – March 2026 · UKAid/MECS funded · with Dr. Sayli Bhide & Prof. Jayendran Venkateswaran (IEOR, IITB)

Developed a sustainable supply ecosystem for electric cooking appliances in rural Bihar. Activated 10 blocks through a supply-chain facilitation model; reached 12,000+ households with 1,505 paying customers converting to electric cooking. Led field operations, IoT data collection (10M+ records), nudging experiments (n=194), and co-authored the public MECS Phase II report.

Sustainable Access to Reliable Energy Services: Super-Efficient Appliances & Storage
January 2021 – 2022 · RCT collaboration with UC Berkeley / UC Santa Barbara / EESL · with Dr. Sayli Bhide, Prof. Jayendran Venkateswaran, Prof. Ranjit Deshmukh (UC Santa Barbara), Prof. Meredith Fowlie (UC Berkeley)

Analysed RCT data on super-efficient appliances and Li-ion battery storage for low-income rural households. Findings: 70–80% reduction in household energy use and up to 80% reduction in electricity bills. Results fed into national subsidy policy recommendations.

Sustained Use of LPG and Solar Induction Cookstoves for Cleaner Cooking in Rural India
January 2021 – · with Dr. Sayli Bhide, Prof. Jayendran Venkateswaran (IEOR, IITB), Prof. Praveen Kumar (Boston College)

Studying social, economic, technological, and behavioural factors that drive or hinder the sustained adoption of LPG and solar induction cookstoves over traditional biomass. Multi-round data collection across UP, Bihar, and Assam.

SoULS — Solar Urja through Localization for Sustainability
November 2017 – 2022 · with Dr. Rohit Sharma (VIT Bhopal), Dr. Sayli Bhide, Prof. Jayendran Venkateswaran & Prof. Chetan Singh Solanki (IEOR, IITB)

Analytics owner for the world's largest community-based solar lamp programme (6M+ lamps across multiple Indian states). Built 4-wave panel data models on 1,159 households (WTP, women's empowerment, education outcomes), applied clustering and regression, and administered REDCap-based multi-state surveys.
